01 // GULF COAST CLIMATOLOGY & THERMODYNAMICS

HOUSTON DOESN’T GIVE YOUR HVAC
MUCH OF A BREAK.

Between 98°F dry-bulb temperatures, 85% Gulf moisture saturation, and a grueling 10-month cooling demand, air conditioners in South Texas work harder than almost anywhere in North America.
01.01 // LATENT HEAT

Extreme Humidity Load

In Houston, cooling the air is only half the battle. Your unit must condense gallons of airborne water every hour. Oversized systems cool too fast, leaving homes clammy and inviting microbial growth.

Ron's Check: Latent vs Sensible capacity calculation.
01.02 // ATTIC AMBIENT

140°F Attic Enclosures

Flex ductwork routed through unconditioned Houston attics operates in blistering thermal zones. Kinked runs, poor mastic seals, and choked plenums rob up to 35% of total system cooling efficiency.

Ron's Check: External static pressure (ESP) audit.
01.03 // ELECTRICAL WEAR

Premature Component Burnout

Surge spikes, severe vibration, and continuous run hours fatigue dual-run capacitors and pit contactor points. Often, a system condemned as “dead” simply has an inexpensive electrical failure.

Ron's Check: Microfarad capacitance & in-rush testing.
